docs/ALGORITHM-SPEC.md: formulas, signals, model specifications and process.

M0 Freeze contracts and ownership.
M1 Collect overall-clip and original-timeline feedback with review context.
M2 Freeze a reviewed benchmark and measure the current detector.
M3 Reuse observations/features and evaluate predictions without rendering videos.
M4 Compare tracked-player and temporal-rule candidates, then explicitly promote.
M5 Enforce NAS/local retention and recovery before unattended experiments.
M6 Add optional AI annotation assistance after measuring agreement and review time.

The lab owns formulas, signals, model specifications and evaluation procedure/code.
NAS server owns shared research records and scheduling; independent workers compute;
website owns review interaction and its existing likes/courts/preferences.

Model training is deferred. Keep private recordings, annotations, locations,
configuration and model artifacts outside public Git repositories.
